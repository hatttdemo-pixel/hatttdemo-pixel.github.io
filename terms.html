Terms of use

Starlink ITC Azure Trial is an independent personal research tool maintained by hatttdemo-pixel. This website explains the tool; it does not offer a public hosted collection service.

Purpose and authorization

The tool archives publicly available satellite ephemeris files into the owner's private Google Drive. Only authorize an account and storage destination you control. Google access is limited to the requested drive.file scope. Data handling is described in the privacy policy.

Research limitations

Availability and accuracy depend on the source, network, server, and Google services. Files may be missing or delayed. The tool does not guarantee complete historical recovery or uninterrupted operation, and archived data should be independently checked before use. It is not intended for safety-critical operational decisions.

Owner control

The owner can stop collection and revoke Google access at any time. Existing files remain until separately deleted by the owner. The project makes no service-level or paid-service commitment. Source data remains subject to the source provider's applicable terms and rights.

Affiliation and changes

This project is not affiliated with or endorsed by SpaceX, Starlink, Google, or Microsoft. Any change to the tool's purpose or requested Google permissions will require an updated explanation and any consent required by Google.
